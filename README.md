# Excel Retail Sales Business Analysis

## Overview

An end-to-end Excel business analysis project analysing retail sales performance across product categories, regions and customer segments.

The project uses Excel PivotTables, PivotCharts and dashboard visualisations to transform raw sales data into clear business insights.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse overall revenue and profit performance
- Compare revenue across product categories
- Identify regional sales differences
- Analyse customer segment performance
- Identify monthly revenue trends
- Calculate profit margin and total orders
- Present findings through an interactive Excel dashboard

## Dashboard

The dashboard provides a high-level view of:

- Total Revenue: £71,615.20
- Total Profit: £30,829.20
- Profit Margin: 43.05%
- Total Orders: 500
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Monthly Revenue Trend

## Excel Techniques Used

- PivotTables

- Data aggregation
- Revenue and profit analysis
